Accuracy boundary / money

No one-man origin story.

Keynes did not invent fiat money, and modern fiat money is not based on him. Monetary systems have a much longer history. Georg Friedrich Knapp formalized a state theory of money associated with chartalism before Keynes’s major macroeconomic work. The 1944 Bretton Woods agreement remained linked to gold for official convertibility.
