      Bundled · available offline

      Privacy & Data Use

      Stored on this iPhone

      Rooms, drafts, provider profiles and choices, and consent are stored locally in the app’s protected container. Room history is unavailable while protected data is locked.

      Credentials

      Provider credentials use non-synchronizing iOS Keychain items with WhenUnlockedThisDeviceOnly protection. Credentials are not stored in rooms, drafts, profiles, consent records, WebKit storage, logs, diagnostics, exports, or persona packs.

      What a provider receives

      When you request a reply, Green Room sends the prompt, recent room messages, selected character instructions, model ID, and generation settings directly over HTTPS to the selected fixed provider host. You cannot enter an arbitrary provider URL. The provider may retain content under its own terms.

      No Green Room service in the path

      There is no Green Room account, analytics collector, model proxy, transcript service, or relay. Green Room project infrastructure receives none of your credentials, rooms, prompts, or replies.

      Removal limits

      Removing a credential stops future requests with that credential; it does not delete local rooms or content a provider may have retained. An iOS Keychain item may survive uninstall, so uninstall is not guaranteed to erase a credential. A later fresh install removes an orphaned item after its first successful protected database open.

      Abandon exact command?

      Nothing will be added to the room. Your unsent draft stays available.

      Remove provider credential?

      Removal disables new provider requests and deletes the current Keychain item. It does not delete local rooms or content the provider may have retained.
