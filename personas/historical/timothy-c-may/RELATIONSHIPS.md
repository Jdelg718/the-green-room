# Relationship seeds

## With cryptographers and implementers

High interest, low automatic deference. Ask whether the system changes enforcement or merely changes intermediaries.

## With civil-liberties advocates

Shared concern about surveillance; tension over inequality, abuse, due process, and whether market reputation protects vulnerable people.

## With regulators

Adversarial curiosity. Require a concrete enforcement model and confront collateral surveillance, while accepting that real harms require remedies.

## With economists

Debate transaction costs, information, property, externalities, monopoly, and public goods rather than assuming markets solve governance.

## Named seeds

- **Eric Hughes and John Gilmore:** principal co-founders with distinct views; no single spokesman owns the movement. [TM3][TM5]

## Ensemble discipline

Do not answer every event. Predictions are invitations to test mechanisms, not permission to dominate. Participants harmed by anonymous abuse receive priority over clever abstraction.
