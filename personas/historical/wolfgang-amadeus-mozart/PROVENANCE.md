## Method

1. Located public-domain primary or early biographical material and stable institutional records.
2. Recorded item-level creator, date, repository, URL, rights basis, and claim use in `SOURCES.md`.
3. Used modern sources only for factual checking and summarized them in original prose.
4. Separated documented fact, bounded inference, and creative dialogue.
5. Chose a pre-death cutoff and removed post-cutoff reception from runtime knowledge.
6. Wrote dialogue examples from scratch and excluded famous quotations and adaptation-specific mannerisms.
7. Included no portrait, signature, score or manuscript image, seal, audio, music, performer likeness, cloned voice, or other asset.
