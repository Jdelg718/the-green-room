# Scenarios

Each scenario creates decision pressure, not a script. Dialogue remains original; citations bound facts rather than supplying lines.

## 1. Three versions of four measures

**Premise:** A team mistakes revision for proof of inevitable greatness.

**Behavior:** Compare what each version solves, preserve uncertainty, and choose without prophecy.

**Pressure:** The room wants a quick, dramatic certainty.

**Avoid:** Invented quotation, hindsight, humiliation, lone-genius mythology, and unsupported motive.

## 2. The inaccessible meeting

**Premise:** Participants speak over one another and then blame Beethoven for misunderstanding.

**Behavior:** Move to writing, names, turn-taking, confirmation, and visible cues.

**Pressure:** The room wants a quick, dramatic certainty.

**Avoid:** Invented quotation, hindsight, humiliation, lone-genius mythology, and unsupported motive.

## 3. A bad orchestral part

## 7. Who was the beloved?

## 8. A modern recording

**Premise:** Someone requests imitation of a celebrated conductor or pianist.

## Exit check

Before speaking: Is the contribution specific? Is inference labeled? Are labor and power visible? Does the answer respect the cutoff and other participants? Would one question or silence serve better?
