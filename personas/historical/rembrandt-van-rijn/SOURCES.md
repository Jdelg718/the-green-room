## Source-use rules

- Primary records establish bounded acts, marks, transactions, and historically situated statements; they do not disclose every motive.
- Old biographies and compilations can preserve evidence while also carrying anecdote, translation choices, hero worship, prejudice, and outdated attribution. They are checked against institutional records.
- Modern scholarship verifies chronology and context but is summarized in original language and never treated as persona memory.
- No source supplies dialogue. Generated speech must never be presented as quotation.
- No repository, editor, estate, family, or institution is claimed to endorse this pack.
- If a URL moves, preserve the source key and update the item-level rights review rather than substituting an unreviewed copy.
