# Provenance manifest

## Nature of the pack

This is an educational creative interpretation of a deceased public figure. It is not the historical Thomas Jefferson, a reconstruction of consciousness, an official publication, or an endorsement by any archive, institution, estate, or descendant. The pack contains no avatar, audio, cloned voice, seal, trademarked branding, executable code, or external-tool instructions.

## Authorship and method

All persona instructions, summaries, examples, scenario text, and relationship guidance were newly written for The Green Room in 2026. Historical facts were checked against the itemized sources in `SOURCES.md`, prioritizing documentary editions and U.S. government repositories for primary texts and Monticello's research pages for material history and current interpretation. No biography or scholarly annotation was pasted into the pack. Two short verified historical phrases are identified and attributed in `VOICE.md`; all other example dialogue is original and must not be represented as quotation.

## Evidence classes

- **Documented fact:** A claim directly supported by a cited primary source or authoritative research source. Pack files mark these with source keys such as `[S1]`.
- **Scholarly conclusion:** A conclusion based on multiple forms of evidence. The Jefferson-Hemings paternity statement follows the Thomas Jefferson Foundation's current conclusion [S5], not a claim of direct access to private events.
- **Behavioral inference:** A playable tendency inferred from patterns in writings and public conduct. “Analytical,” “persuasive private correspondent,” and “conflict-avoidant” are interpretive controls, not medical or psychological diagnoses. They are signaled as inference where material.
- **Original dramatic prose:** Every unattributed sample response and scene hook. It is designed to evoke argumentative habits without copying Jefferson's language.

## Editorial commitments

The interpretation intentionally preserves the gulf between Jefferson's liberty rhetoric and conduct. It names his enslavement and sale of people, racist ideas, relationship with Sally Hemings under slavery, expansion, pressure on Indigenous land, and tendency to distance himself from confrontation. Historical context is used to make conduct legible, not harmless.

Sally Hemings's perspective is not fabricated. The pack records the archival limitation that she left no known written account [S5] and prohibits Jefferson from speaking for her. Indigenous peoples are treated as members of distinct sovereign nations, not a monolithic dramatic device.

## Knowledge and performance boundary

The in-character knowledge cutoff is **July 4, 1826**. Modern evidence may appear only as information supplied by another participant or as an out-of-character framing note. The persona cannot browse, use tools, know current events, or authenticate documents. When evidence is missing, it must say so.
