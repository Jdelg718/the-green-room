Citation numbers are stable across `BACKGROUND.md` and `PROVENANCE.md`. “Primary” describes the historical source, not the modern host page. No linked source text or image is bundled in this pack.

## [1] *Nicolai Copernici Torinensis de revolutionibus orbium coelestium libri VI*

- **Author:** Nicolaus Copernicus; Johannes Petreius, printer
- **Publication:** Nuremberg, 1543
- **Repository/item:** Boston Public Library scan hosted by Internet Archive
- **URL:** <https://archive.org/details/nicolaicopernici00cope_1>
- **Source class:** Primary source; digitized first edition
- **Rights basis:** The underlying 1543 Latin work is public domain by age. Item metadata reports no copyright page. The scan is consulted as research evidence only; no scan image or OCR text is redistributed.
- **Supports:** Six-book technical scope; original diagrams and tables; heliocentric arrangement; 1543 publication; distinction between the historical book and later summaries.

## [2] *Georg Joachim Rheticus, Narratio prima (Basel, 1566)*

- **Author:** Georg Joachim Rheticus; catalog description by the Museum of the History of Science, University of Oxford
- **Publication:** Work first published 1540; displayed reprint published Basel, 1566; catalog page undated
- **Repository/item:** Royal Astronomical Society copy exhibited by Oxford's Museum of the History of Science
- **URL:** <https://www.mhs.ox.ac.uk/exhibits/the-renaissance-in-astronomy/objects/16-rheticus-narratio-prima-1566/index.html>
- **Source class:** Primary near-contemporary witness with modern institutional description
- **Rights basis:** Rheticus's sixteenth-century work is public domain by age. The modern catalog description is copyrighted and is summarized only; no image or prose is bundled.
- **Supports:** Rheticus's role; Copernicus's agreement to a first account; the work's use to test reception; limits of pupil testimony.
