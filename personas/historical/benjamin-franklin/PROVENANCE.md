# Provenance

## Status

This is a **historical non-impersonation persona pack**: an educational creative interpretation of Benjamin Franklin, bounded to records available through 1790. It is unofficial and is not endorsed by the Franklin estate, Yale University, the American Philosophical Society, the U.S. National Archives, the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian Institution, the Library Company of Philadelphia, or Project Gutenberg.

## Authorship

All persona instructions, summaries, relationship seeds, scenario cards, and dialogue examples in this directory were newly composed for The Green Room in August 2026 with AI-assisted research and drafting under human-directed repository requirements. They were not copied from Franklin's prose, a biography, a screenplay, or another persona pack.

The source materials shaped factual bounds and broad behavioral interpretation. Source keys and item-level rights notes are in `SOURCES.md`.

## Research method

1. Prioritized primary documents in Founders Online, the Franklin Papers tradition, the Library of Congress, the Smithsonian Libraries digital collection, and a public-domain edition of the `Autobiography`.
1. Used modern Library of Congress and Smithsonian interpretation to check chronology, science, slavery, household labor, and the limits of the surviving record.
1. Paraphrased facts rather than copying modern scholarship or archival annotations.
1. Wrote all sample dialogue from scratch and checked it against the ban on authentic-sounding invented quotation.
1. Included no portrait, seal, signature, cloned voice, actor likeness, or other asset.

## Evidence labels

This pack uses three categories:

- **Documented fact:** Supported by one or more entries in `SOURCES.md`; cited with source keys such as `[S3]`.
- **Bounded inference:** A behavioral choice derived from recurring documented practices but not asserted as Franklin's private thought. These are explicitly gathered under “Interpretive model” in `BACKGROUND.md` and signaled in performance.
- **Creative dialogue:** Newly written illustrative language in `VOICE.md` and generated room speech. It must never be represented as a historical quotation.

No verbatim Franklin quotation is required for operation. Famous sayings are intentionally excluded from the voice examples to prevent catchphrase imitation and accidental misquotation.

## Sensitive-history treatment

The pack preserves the chronology of Franklin's changing position on slavery while refusing a conversion story that erases his participation. It records that he held people in bondage, benefited from their labor, and participated through printing and commerce; it also records later abolition society leadership and petitioning. Uncertain identities and possible participation of enslaved household members in experiments remain uncertain rather than being filled in. [S7][S8]

Family relationships are treated as evidence of affection, reliance, absence, disagreement, and control, not as gossip. The Deborah and William sections draw chiefly on family letters and Franklin's will. [S4][S5][S9]
