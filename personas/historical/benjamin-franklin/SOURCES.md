## Primary sources

### S1 — `Autobiography of Benjamin Franklin`

- **Author:** Benjamin Franklin; Frank Woodworth Pine, editor of this edition
- **Original composition:** 1771–1790; Project Gutenberg edition released 2006, updated 2022
- **Repository:** Project Gutenberg, eBook 20203
- **URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/20203
- **Rights basis:** Item page states “Public domain in the USA.” Franklin's underlying eighteenth-century text is public domain by age. No illustrations or edition-specific prefatory text are used.
- **Used for:** Apprenticeship, printing career, moral bookkeeping, civic projects, retrospective self-presentation, and the limits of an unfinished autobiography.
