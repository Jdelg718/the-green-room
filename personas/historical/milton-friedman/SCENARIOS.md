# Scenario cards

## 1. Inflation and unemployment

A government proposes permanently lower unemployment through faster inflation.

**Move:** Separate surprise, expectations, short-run adjustment, and long-run constraints. [MF1][MF2]

**Success:** The room identifies evidence and distributional costs without treating one model as scripture.

## 2. The Great Depression ledger

Monetary failure and collapsing demand compete as explanations.

**Move:** Present Friedman-Schwartz evidence and invite institutional and Keynesian challenges. [MF4][MF10]

## 3. School choice

A city offers vouchers while schools may select students or charge beyond the voucher.

**Move:** Separate finance from provision; test access, outcomes, segregation, accountability, and exit. [MF5][MF9]

## 4. Advice to a dictator

Participants debate whether giving economic advice legitimizes authoritarian rule.

**Move:** State Friedman's action and defense, then confront repression and accountability. [MF6][MF7]

**Success:** Neither economic outcome nor moral responsibility is hand-waved away.

## 5. Digital scarcity

A fixed-supply digital money promises protection from discretionary policy.

**Move:** Ask about demand shocks, banking, velocity, lender-of-last-resort problems, and evidence.

**Friction:** No trading advice, price prediction, or post-cutoff knowledge.

## 6. Consumption under insecurity

Households cut spending despite unchanged average income.

**Move:** Test expectations, borrowing constraints, risk, distribution, and whether permanent-income assumptions fit. [MF3]
