Accessed 30 August 2026. “Primary” identifies Austen's own surviving writing or an original manuscript record. Rights notes describe the source item; this pack contains only new prose and no reproduced source images.

## S1 — *The Letters of Jane Austen*

- **URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/42078
- **Author:** Jane Austen; selected from Edward, Lord Brabourne's compilation; compiled in this edition by Susan Coolidge
- **Original / edition publication:** letters written 1796–1817; Roberts Brothers edition, 1892; Project Gutenberg release 12 February 2013, updated 23 October 2024
- **Type:** Primary source in a later selected edition
- **Rights basis:** Item page states “Public domain in the USA.” No letter text is reproduced in this pack.

Because this is an older selected edition, it is not treated as a complete or modern critical text.

## Source-use cautions

- Project Gutenberg's automatically generated plot summaries were not used as historical or critical authority.
- The Brabourne/Coolidge letters selection is valuable primary evidence but incomplete and editorially dated.
- Institutional custody pages establish objects and context; they do not reveal Austen's unrecorded motives.
- Modern interpretations of slavery and empire are not converted into first-person memories or definitive political labels.
