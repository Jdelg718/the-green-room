# Sources

Accessed **2026-08-30**. Source keys are stable within this pack. “Rights basis” records item-level status or the limited research use made here. Modern catalogue prose, translations, transcriptions, and images are not imported into the pack.

## Primary manuscripts, drawings, and public-domain edition

### S1 — `Arundel MS 263: Leonardo da Vinci, Notebook (The Codex Arundel)`

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci; later assembled from loose papers
- **Date:** 1478–1518; most sheets date from around 1508
- **Repository:** British Library, Archives and Manuscripts, Arundel MS 263
- **URL:** https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/040-002039546
- **Rights basis:** Leonardo's manuscript content is public domain by age. The catalogue provides full digital coverage through a IIIF viewer, but British Library images and modern catalogue text may have separate terms. No image, transcription, or catalogue prose is redistributed.
- **Used for:** The heterogeneous, later-assembled nature of a surviving codex; mirror writing; diagrams and notes on mechanics, optics, mathematics, astronomy, water, accounts, and personal memoranda; the danger of treating the papers as one ordered diary.

### S2 — `Notebooks of Leonardo da Vinci, vol. I (Codex Forster I)`

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci
- **Date:** About 1487–1490 and 1505; present object combines two notebooks
- **Repository:** Victoria and Albert Museum, National Art Library, MSL/1876/Forster/141/I
- **URL:** https://collections.vam.ac.uk/item/O1380839/notebooks-of-leonardo-da-vinci-manuscript-da-vinci-leonardo/
- **Rights basis:** Leonardo's manuscript is public domain by age. The V&A page gives limited terms for its digital images; no image is included here. Modern object-record prose and bibliography are summarized, not copied.
- **Used for:** Notebook material and scale; hydraulic devices, posture and movement, art practice, anatomy, architecture, and varied subjects; binding history; Francesco Melzi and later provenance; drawing and note-taking as connected work.

### S3 — `Codex Atlanticus`

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci; sheets later gathered in their present collection
- **Date:** Late fifteenth to early sixteenth century; institutional page publication date not stated
- **Repository:** Veneranda Biblioteca Ambrosiana
- **URL:** https://www.ambrosiana.it/en/discover/masterpieces/codex-atlanticus/
- **Rights basis:** Leonardo's underlying drawings and writings are public domain by age. Ambrosiana's digital images, captions, and site content may carry separate rights; none is redistributed.
- **Used for:** The range of surviving studies—water machinery, manufacture, geometry, architecture, fortifications, weapons, flight, maps, horses, and spectacle—and the rule that a sketch does not prove construction, performance, novelty, or deployment.

### S4 — `The aortic valve` (RCIN 919082)

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci
- **Date:** About 1512–1513
- **Repository:** Royal Collection Trust
- **URL:** https://www.rct.uk/collection/919082/the-aortic-valve
- **Rights basis:** Leonardo's drawing and notes are public domain by age. The Royal Collection page marks its image copyright and credits modern catalogue text to Martin Clayton and Ron Philo; no image, transcription, or modern prose is copied.
- **Used for:** A direct surviving sheet joining anatomical drawing, mould and glass-model planning, flow observation, geometrical analogy, and error; evidence that visual reasoning was iterative rather than infallible.

### S5 — `The Notebooks of Leonardo Da Vinci — Complete`

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci; translated and thematically edited by Jean Paul Richter
- **Publication date:** 1888; Project Gutenberg eBook 5000 released 2004, updated 2026
- **Repository:** Project Gutenberg
- **URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/5000
- **Rights basis:** The item page states “Public domain in the USA.” The nineteenth-century translation and arrangement are not copied into persona dialogue.
- **Used for:** Public-domain access to selected writings; recurring attention to painting, perspective, anatomy, mechanics, water, and unfinished plans for ordered treatises; the caution that this is a later translated compilation, not Leonardo's own continuous book.

### S10 — `Studies of water` (RCIN 912660)

- **Creator:** Leonardo da Vinci
- **Date:** About 1510–1512
- **Repository:** Royal Collection Trust
- **URL:** https://www.rct.uk/collection/912660/studies-of-water
- **Rights basis:** Leonardo's underlying sheet is public domain by age. The page marks images as copyrighted and supplies modern catalogue interpretation; no image or source wording is reused.
- **Used for:** Repeatable flow around obstacles, falling water, eddies, bubbles and waves; the Arno survey and canal plan; close observation alongside the unrealized effort to organize many cases into general principles.

## Source-use rules

- `[S1]` through `[S5]` and `[S10]` point to primary manuscripts, drawings, or a public-domain translation of primary material: **six primary-source entries**.
- `[S1]` through `[S12]` include the British Library, V&A, Biblioteca Ambrosiana, Royal Collection Trust, National Gallery, Metropolitan Museum of Art, Museo Galileo, Project Gutenberg, and a named academic historian: **twelve researched entries**, all summarized in original language.
- Primary material establishes surviving marks and historically bounded statements; it does not reveal every motive or make a later arrangement into Leonardo's intended system.
- Modern scholarship checks chronology, attribution, and context. It is not copied, and disagreement or uncertainty is preserved.
- No source supplies persona dialogue. All voice examples and generated speech must remain original and must never be presented as authentic quotation.
- No source or repository is claimed to endorse this pack.
- If a link moves, update the URL and access date without silently changing the source key or rights analysis.
