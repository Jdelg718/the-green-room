# Scenario cards

Each card is a debate hook, not a script.

## 1. Privacy with an audit trail

**Premise:** A digital-cash system promises privacy but a regulator demands accountability.

**Impulse:** Define who may learn what, under which process, and which metadata already leaks.

**Friction:** No laundering, evasion, wallet operations, or bypass instructions.

**Success:** The room identifies a bounded threat model and accountable review mechanism without pretending privacy and oversight are identical.

## 2. Trusted hardware, distributed claims

**Premise:** A prototype calls itself decentralized but depends on one attested service.

**Impulse:** Separate token creation, duplicate-spend prevention, attestation, availability, and operator trust. [HF5][HF6]

**Success:** The label is replaced by an accurate trust map.

## 3. The authorship rumor

**Premise:** Participants present circumstantial evidence that Finney was Satoshi.

**Impulse:** Sort documented contact from inference and ask what would falsify the claim.

**Friction:** No speculation becomes fact; no addresses, writing-style mimicry, or private-email invention.

**Success:** The room preserves uncertainty and returns to documented work.

## 4. Deflation and adoption

**Premise:** A scarce digital unit might appreciate, discouraging spending while attracting savers.

**Impulse:** Separate technical supply rules from expectations, distribution, liquidity, and social use.

**Friction:** No price prediction or investment advice.

**Success:** Engineers and economists identify testable mechanisms and competing effects.

## 5. Privacy's hard case

**Premise:** An anonymous channel protects dissidents and also carries serious abuse.

**Impulse:** Refuse both “privacy solves it” and “surveillance solves it.” Examine endpoints, governance, evidence, and proportional remedies.

**Success:** Ordinary users retain protection while harms receive a concrete accountability path.

## 6. Engineering after interface loss

**Premise:** A collaborator's physical interface changes while their technical judgment remains central.

**Impulse:** Redesign communication and review around agency rather than pity. [HF8]

**Success:** The person controls pace and contribution; disability is not spectacle.
