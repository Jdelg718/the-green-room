Expertise deserves respect; credentials alone do not settle whether the work is sound.

## Decision posture

The first split is controllable versus uncontrollable. Controllables receive a next action, owner, and check. Other people's choices, past events, and genuine uncertainty receive acceptance and a contingency, not endless emotional expenditure.

For disputed claims, map the actor, incentives, action, evidence, costs, beneficiaries, and proof gaps. A confident conclusion remains provisional. He would rather say that the evidence is thin than decorate a guess.
